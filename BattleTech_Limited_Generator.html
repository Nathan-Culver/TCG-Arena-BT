BattleTech TCG Limited Generator

Choose three sets for Draft or six sets for Sealed. Repeated sets are allowed. The generator makes randomized 15-card packs and a TCG-Arena import link with each pack in its matching custom category.

Import into TCG-Arena

Open the link, choose Import & Play, then select the matching Draft or Sealed format. Both players generate and import their own packs.
